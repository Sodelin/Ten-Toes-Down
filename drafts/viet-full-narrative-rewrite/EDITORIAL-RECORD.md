## Editorial brief applied across the series

The task was a complete narrative rewrite, retaining the source's full chapter and scene progression, family chronology, relationships, motives, major action, and endings. The writers rebuilt narration, dialogue, scene transitions, and physical blocking. Distinctive source jokes and short wording remain where they still work; this was not a requirement to erase every recognizable sentence.

The broad *Gintama* craft reference concerns affectionate absurdity, ridiculous physical action, social embarrassment, and room for sincerity within the same story. It does not supply borrowed characters, scenes, or dialogue. The books keep the pleasure of food, clothes, money, gifts, family rivalry, and ludicrous strength alongside real injuries and deaths. Serious stretches are allowed to breathe.

The narrators' knowledge has limits. Observations, remembered events, recordings, reports, and guesses should be distinguishable. Spoken turns receive a named speaker, clear speech tag, or identifying action in their own paragraph for one audiobook narrator. These are editorial intentions supported by manual review and mechanical screens, not proof that every line is beyond further correction.

The rewrite preserves criminal choices and their consequences. Aiden enjoys paid intimidation and status; Minh accepts money and favors; Tín's coalition includes people who profited from wrongdoing. Cooperation does not retrospectively make them innocent. Shawna, Mai, Bích, Hương, and the other women retain their work, decisions, anger, affection, and boundaries. Book IV's affair remains deliberate deception between unrelated adults, and Shawna's separation and independent business success are not replaced by reconciliation as a prize for Aiden's heroics.

Word counts diagnose compression rather than certify literary completeness. Books II and III received substantial additional scene work after their first passes compressed too many exchanges. Book IV also restored quiet conversations and practical transitions during its breadth review. There was no shorter-novel target. Per-book notes and count tables document the methods and remaining differences from the source.

### Narrative and listening

First-person Aiden remains the narrator. Speaker changes are attributed with speech tags or actions in the same paragraph. Each scene gains movement through a located room or street; character opinions remain Aiden's impressions rather than unexplained access to other minds. The rewriting is scene-by-scene authored prose, not a global dialogue-tag replacement.

Example: the original moves directly from the mistaken-identity opening to the gift total. This gives the crowd business to perform before the next speaker arrives.

Example: the source's brief clipboard fold becomes a grasp, downward pull, release, knee brace, break, and return. The two versions preserve the same outcome; the rewrite lets a listener locate hands and bodies without inventing the movement.
