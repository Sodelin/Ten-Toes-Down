# The Greatest Man Alive — edition record

Written by ChatGPT for Nolan, following Nolan's creative direction. September 2026.

## Scope and approach

This is a new six-book Ten Toes Down edition. It expands the Vegas origin into a completed action and romance epic, with a final confrontation and a return home. The earlier 200-page Blood Money and Blue Diamonds edition and the earlier experiments remain separate and preserved.

The direction was sincere, excessive criminal-cop fanfiction: Aiden is absurdly admired and competent, his friends have their own ambitions, and love and loyalty drive the confrontations. The voice uses repeated familiar profanity inside recognition, arguments, boasts, interruptions, and affection. The new edition adds actual scenes and adventures; the page count does not come from blank pages or repetition of an earlier manuscript.

The six movements are Vegas and the valedictorian captaincy; the adult Sky High adventure and an alternate-history rescue; Monaco's Grand Prix of Kings; the World Championship of Everything and Saint's vault; the Crown of Midnight; and the Last City confrontation followed by homecoming, businesses, marriages, and an ending.

Recollections, testimony, courtship stories, and letters are embedded inside the main narrative. The structural influences were named by Nolan: Frankenstein, The King in Yellow, and The Count of Monte Cristo. This edition does not adapt the Jerry Lewis film mentioned in the conversation. Its 1944 mission takes place in an explicitly altered fictional branch and does not claim to change the historical Holocaust. The people being rescued have names, relationships, expertise, and choices.

## Continuity decisions

Aiden and Shawna's marriage continues through the later adventures. The core ensemble remains alive at the ending; prior deaths are not reversed. Aiden becomes an actual police captain during the Book I graduation episode. Graduation is one episode in the larger story.

The DBM engraving now means “Damn, boy. Magnificent.” It began as Dante's boast and becomes Aiden's affectionate claim. The old engraving explanation is superseded only in this new edition.

Blue Thunder begins only at the end of Book II. Its use is brief and exhausting; it does not confer unlimited flight, healing, time travel, or power to rewrite the world. The single historical passage is closed. The dark blue police Lamborghini, Tank's original black Mercedes, and later prize cars are distinguished. Crown, diamond, ledger, letters, and other important objects have explicit custody at handoffs. Similar champion names were separated where they risked confusion.

Three additional adventures join Book V after its auction episode, and an additional homecoming episode joins Book VI before the weddings. The assembled reading edition renumbers chapters continuously inside each book.

## Reading formats and verification

The PDF is a 6-by-9-inch reading edition with embedded fonts, a linked table of contents, book and chapter bookmarks, running book titles, and page numbers. The EPUB contains separately navigable chapters and supports reader-controlled type size. The Markdown is the editable text.

Final counts and checks are recorded below after assembly. Word counts use Python whitespace splitting and include the stated headings and front matter. These are transparent file counts, not a claim that every formatting convention would count words identically.

The checks cover chapter sequence and completeness, long exact duplicate paragraphs, control characters, rendered chapter openings and endings, PDF navigation and selected visual pages, and EPUB XML, manifest, reading order, and links. Cross-book handoffs and the principal deaths, relationships, powers, and objects received targeted editorial review. This is not a claim that a separate human editor has read every line or that every page received individual visual inspection. Humor and literary effect remain matters for readers to judge.

Draft checkpoints were committed while the books were in progress. The final publication preserves earlier editions and records exact published file hashes.
