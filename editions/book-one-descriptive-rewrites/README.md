## The editorial direction

Aiden remembers the past through continuous narrative. The prose establishes the room, the people, their intentions, what they do, what changes, and how it feels. Action and dialogue remain understandable when one person reads every line. Humor comes from what happens in a scene as well as the descriptions and reactions.

Version A gives more space to remembered relationships and the sweep of the story. Version B emphasizes sensory experience, movement, injury, and aftermath. Both retain the source plot and major comic payoffs so the comparison concerns the storytelling.
