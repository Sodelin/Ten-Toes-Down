# The Second Great Butch vs. Stud War

## The Ten Toes Down Panorama of Games for Lillian - Coalition Edition

**240 distinct games, six volumes, 601 PDF pages.**

Aiden buys a triumphal arch before anyone decides who won. Butches, studs, drag performers, lesbians and the trans delegation negotiate a game-night coalition while veterans of the entirely fictional 1967 Blue Comet Social Club feud return with incompatible memories, an old jacket and a peace agreement signed on opposite sides of an invoice. The caucuses overlap; the characters have individual rivalries, friendships and romances.

This is an adult comic edition: strong profanity, non-hateful reclaimed in-group dialogue, party and cocaine references, consensual sexual innuendo and an intentionally excessive crew chorus. The invented 1967 war is not a claim about real LGBTQ history.

## The Department of Lesbian Lore Has Seized the Microphone

The new field guide has 22 sourced historical dossiers, 6,546 words including reading scopes and original comic interludes, and 32 linked historical source records. It begins in Volume 1 on page 106. Use its internal index, the PDF bookmarks, the EPUB contents, or the offline reader's dedicated historical section.

It follows several locally specific histories from ancient reception and nineteenth-century diaries through Black lesbian culture, organizing, sexual politics, community archives and later international and diasporic networks. It does not claim a complete global history or a single developmental ladder. Historical prose, evidence limits and fictional summit scenes are visibly separate.

## What this edition changes

All 240 comic scenes, case titles and verdicts have been rewritten, along with each volume's framing and the front matter. All non-fiction game fields, including the 720 plain summary fields, have been compared by exact value with the Tellings Cut and preserved. The inspected evidence cache is unchanged.

The user requested at least fifteen occurrences of the reclaimed plural on every page. A fictional Aiden-and-Tank chorus supplies fifteen on every fixed PDF page, including covers, indexes and credits; every final page is checked by text extraction. Additional dialogue can raise a page's count. The chorus has its own footer band. Reflowable EPUB has no fixed page size, so its chorus appears at each chapter boundary.

The manuscript has 161,683 words including headings, source notes and repeated chapter choruses. The separately measured comic/catalogue prose and rules total 112,501 words and exclude the presentation chorus. These are six catalogue volumes, not six 50,000-word novels.

The earlier Panorama and Tellings Cut remain unchanged. The source novels are not edited; the new coalition cast, wars and romances are companion fiction.

## Choosing a game

A place to start means an editorially promising candidate. Read the catch first identifies a meaningful obstacle. A more demanding detour is a narrower or harder possibility. The 240 entries retain 33 promising candidates, 188 conditional candidates and 19 detours. The optional dice route never overrides a real preference, and every refusal remains free.

Developer-declaration filters exclude missing evidence. Mouse-only can still require dragging, and turn-based games can still involve difficult menus. Read the exact edition and practical catch. No full playthrough, personal fit, physical print or particular e-reader test is claimed.

## Print and rebuild

PDFs use Letter paper and embedded fonts. Print at actual size or fit to A4; individual volumes are easier to handle than the omnibus. EPUB permits larger text and reflow.

The self-contained source directory contains the JSON books, front matter, sanitized evidence records and builders. From source/, run python work/build_panorama.py, python work/build_panorama_finder.py and python work/validate_panorama.py. Dependencies: reportlab, pypdf, pypdfium2 and Pillow. Fonts default to Windows Georgia/Arial; PANORAMA_FONT_DIR can select equivalent licensed files. Run node work/test_panorama_finder.cjs for the routing checks. Rebuilds appear under outputs/ten-toes-down-panorama/.
