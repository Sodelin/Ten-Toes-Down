# Afterword: The Bell Street Rewrite

This is a completely new alternate telling developed by Codex under Nolan’s creative direction. It keeps the four Viet Telling titles and the requested generational structure, while replacing the earlier edition’s prose, setting and central plots. Representative opening passages from the Niggatorial Tellings, including Aiden’s funeral and later-book openings, informed dialogue timing, confidence and comic action. This is not a claim to reproduce every scene or the full continuity of those six books.

The new city, Eastbridge, and its Bell Street neighborhood are invented. The syndicates, club, contests, families and battles are fictional. This is an English-language diaspora crime fantasy; its characters’ slang and extravagant self-chosen nicknames belong to particular relationships. Their speech, family ritual and criminal titles do not describe a universal Vietnamese custom. The original research references for naming and family address were the [SBS Cultural Atlas naming guide](https://culturalatlas.sbs.com.au/vietnamese-culture/vietnamese-culture-naming) and [Northern Illinois University’s kinship lesson](https://seasite.niu.edu/vietnamese/uniLesson8/L8_grammar.htm).

The family line is Tín → Hòa → Mai → Aiden. Minh is Aiden’s father. Tín is alive at 104 in Book I’s 2026 action, 105 in its 2027 ending, and 106 in Book IV. He calls out both parents and performs his own impossible feats. Hòa has a separate, deliberately unrevealed history; no final ranking between him and Tín is supplied.

Book IV’s club brothers, Aiden and Khải, are unrelated consenting adults. Their affair and Aiden’s lies have consequences for Shawna, whose work, choices and separation continue independently of the criminal victory. Intimacy remains nongraphic.

Aiden’s proof and practically useful P=NP machine belong to this alternate world. So do the family’s physical feats and extraordinary longevity. They are devices of the action fantasy, not real techniques or claims that a mathematical proof automatically opens arbitrary computer systems.

This rewrite contains 205,636 narrative words across four completed novels. Counts use whitespace-separated narrative text, excluding Markdown headings, scene dividers and notes. The final copyedit includes independent continuity reads and corrections to vehicle, ship, ladder and rescue movements. Listening segmentation preserves paragraph order and text; native imports were checked after conversion and after the final ladder correction.

The [first edition is preserved at commit 5062f55](https://github.com/Sodelin/Ten-Toes-Down/tree/5062f5579c8b0bdb3376e9c49733399f53b34dd2/editions/the-viet-telling). Whether the new family becomes part of the wider Ten Toes Down continuity remains Nolan’s decision.
