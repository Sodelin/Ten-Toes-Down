# Afterword: Inheritance and Invention

*The Viet Telling* is a noncanonical alternate-story experiment made for the creator of *Ten Toes Down*, developed with Codex from the requested premise. Its point of contact with the original is Aiden: his self-certainty, direct speech, willingness to take the impossible personally, and fierce allegiance to people who need rescuing.

The source reading for this experiment was limited to opening excerpts: approximately 230 lines from the latest edition's first book, first chapter, and approximately 100 lines from book six, first chapter. The source snapshot is [Ten Toes Down commit 3f16cb8](https://github.com/Sodelin/Ten-Toes-Down/tree/3f16cb8dc89171439167297d744e623d6b16df25), in `editions/niggatorial-tellings`. This is an alternate telling informed by those passages, not a claim to reproduce the full series or its complete continuity.

The distance is deliberate and the resemblance partial. The original's first-person certainty, profane banter, swagger and impossible confidence carry into a new family and crime saga, with violent consequences and sustained domestic drama. Nolan's high-level references included generational escalation in Baki, an epic's breadth, and the interlocking family and criminal interests of ensemble crime television. The four novels use original scenes and conflicts. The first and fourth follow Aiden; the second belongs to his father; the third belongs to his maternal great-grandfather. Tín is the father of Hòa, who is the father of Aiden's mother, Mai. Tín remains alive at 104–106 in the 2026–28 stories and calls out both parents. Hòa's unshown past and unexplained strength deliberately leave his ranking open.

This edition contains four new Markdown novels and a combined Markdown manuscript. The original manuscripts remain intact. Whether this alternate family becomes part of the larger Ten Toes Down continuity remains Nolan's choice. Book IV's sworn club brothers are unrelated consenting adults; the affair is a choice with consequences for Aiden, Khải and Shawna, whose own work and decisions continue throughout the story.

The family is invented, as are its enemies, obligations and feats. Vietnamese identity belongs to the people who work, eat, argue, love and make choices here. Their extraordinary abilities and Tín's continued strength at 106 belong to the action fantasy.

## Six Research Notes

1. **The criminal world.** The Global Initiative Against Transnational Organized Crime's 2025 Vietnam profile describes multiple criminal groups and networks, including localized extortion, coercive lending, illegal gambling and connections with legitimate businesses. Those broad patterns informed the fictional underworld. A triad is not simply a Vietnamese synonym for mafia; the term has a particular Chinese and Hong Kong history. None of this story's organizations is presented as a documentary portrait of an actual syndicate. Sources: [Vietnam country profile, Global Organized Crime Index 2025](https://ocindex.net/assets/downloads/2025/english/ocindex_profile_vietnam_2025.pdf); [Hong Kong triads: the historical and political evolution of urban criminal polity, 1842-2020, Urban History](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0963926821001024).

2. **Names.** Vietnamese names generally place the family name before the middle and given names. English-language and diaspora contexts may use a different arrangement, which makes Aiden Trần a plausible American usage beside Trần Quang Minh. Women generally retain their own legal surnames after marriage; Lê Thanh Mai's name therefore does not need to change to match her husband's. The specific names and biographies here are fictional composites. Source: [Vietnamese naming, SBS Cultural Atlas](https://culturalatlas.sbs.com.au/vietnamese-culture/vietnamese-culture-naming).

3. **Family and address.** Vietnamese kinship vocabulary also helps people address one another beyond the immediate family. *Anh* and *em* express older-brother and younger-sibling relationships; a child speaking to parents can refer to himself as *con*. These relationships informed the dialogue, which is rendered principally in English. Aiden's bow belongs to this family's private discipline. It is not presented as a universal Vietnamese homecoming ritual, and respectful address is not a fixed system of criminal ranks. Source: [Kinship terms and personal reference, Northern Illinois University](https://seasite.niu.edu/vietnamese/uniLesson8/L8_grammar.htm).

4. **The living city.** Motorbike traffic, small plastic street-food chairs, late meals, quiet cafés, older buildings and modern towers provide ordinary texture around the violence. The national tourism authority itself uses both Ho Chi Minh City and Saigon. These details ground the present-day setting; they do not mean every neighborhood, household or decade has the same character. Source: [Ho Chi Minh City's contemporary urban life, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://www.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/vibrant-metropolis-ho-chi-minh-city%E2%80%99s-modern-pulse-nightlife-culture).

5. **Water and freight.** Saigon Newport's terminal material documents the large-scale container infrastructure at Cát Lái, including yards, cranes and barge berths. That physical vocabulary informs the industrial waterfront. The story's particular yards, criminal control and ferry-town geography are inventions; the earlier books are not accounts of historical criminal incidents or battles. Source: [Saigon Newport Corporation terminal presentation](https://saigonnewport.com.vn/uploads/files/2021/10/13/1110-ppt-tcty-vie-compressed-1.pdf).

6. **The impossible mathematics.** P versus NP concerns the relationship between problems whose solutions can be efficiently checked and those whose solutions can be efficiently found. Proving P = NP would not, by itself, supply an instantly usable tool for entering any computer: practical algorithms, their running times and the particular system would still matter. Aiden's operative solver and its extreme capabilities are fantasy, as are the family's physical feats. The mathematical language gives those powers their voice; it does not certify them as real techniques. Source: [P vs NP, Clay Mathematics Institute](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/p-vs-np/).
