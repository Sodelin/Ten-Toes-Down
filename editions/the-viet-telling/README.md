# Ten Toes Down: The Viet Telling

**Bell Street rewrite — four complete English novels.**

**205,636 narrative words · 111 chapters · at least 50,000 words in every book.**

Aiden comes home to Bell Street, joins the Wharf Circle, and discovers how much money a city will hand a man who can throw its furniture. His father’s story takes him back to a criminal auction and an eighteen-day wedding countdown. His living great-grandfather wins a floating nightclub and fights a harbor empire. Aiden’s return in Book IV brings the Ninth Room, a stolen-prize race, an affair, and a marriage he can no longer talk his way through.

Eastbridge, California, is an invented port city. This new telling follows a Vietnamese family in a mixed neighborhood, with profane banter, criminal ambition, outrageous gifts, impossible fights and family comedy. Tín remains alive, roasts both parents, and outclasses Minh. Hòa’s unexplained past and ceiling remain deliberately open.

## Edition record

Creative direction: Nolan. New prose: Codex. This is a noncanonical alternate telling, rewritten and copyedited on 13 September 2026. The four titles remain; all narrative prose was replaced for this Bell Street edition.

The [first Viet edition remains available in Git history](https://github.com/Sodelin/Ten-Toes-Down/tree/5062f5579c8b0bdb3376e9c49733399f53b34dd2/editions/the-viet-telling) and in its [separate Google Docs archive](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/10eLOm1a5zyVfR04hj4rj3cwfLMqucK6T). The other story editions remain intact.

Copyediting covered dialogue, chronology, family relationships, injuries, character fates and physical movement in the fights. All 74 imported native documents were read back against the final conversion text. Narrative counts exclude heading lines, scene dividers and these notes. No duplicated paragraphs of 25 or more words or exact first-edition prose reuse passed the final checks.
