# Ten Toes Down: The Viet Telling

**Four complete English novels · 207,527 prose words · 98 chapters.** Each novel exceeds 50,000 prose words.

An alternate crime saga: Aiden joins the Wharf Circle, his father fights his way out of an older criminal empire, his great-grandfather faces the river kingdom he helped build, and Aiden enters the Ninth Room—where a sworn brother, an affair, and a struggle for control threaten the life he has made.

The family grows more dangerous as its stories unfold. Great-grandfather Tín remains alive in the present-day books, formidable at 104–106 and unafraid to call out Minh and Mai. Grandpa Hòa's unexplained past and strength leave his standing beside Tín deliberately open.
