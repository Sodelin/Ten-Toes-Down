## Audio editorial strategy

Use voice selection and pauses to make the existing text easier to follow without changing any words. Keep Aiden conversational, the ticket man dry, Tank booming and sincere, Shawna confident, Mercedes warm and direct, and the guest overeager. Let the original punch lines land, keep scene narration clear, and leave the source’s chronology and explanations exactly where the author placed them. Keep background sound out of this pilot so the dialogue stays easy to hear.

## Performance guide — not spoken

- **AIDEN:** The sole narrator. Same voice for his dialogue. Conversational, mildly offended, clear about the action.
- **TICKET:** Flat and bureaucratic. Treat the unusual situation as routine.
- **TANK:** Booming confidence, including on the phone.
- **SHAWNA:** Confident, precise, affectionate, in charge.
- **MERCEDES:** Warm and direct; distinct from Shawna.
- **GUEST:** Eager and casually opportunistic.
