# Book 1, Chapters 2–4: new dialogue IDs

## Chapter 2

- **BIG_T** — Existing global ID. Man; crew leader who brings Aiden into the family and calls him son. Firm, charismatic authority with real paternal warmth; later dialogue should harden without losing their emotional history.
- **B01C02_CREW_MEMBER_1** — Gender not independently specified; one of the twelve crew members. Brief command during Aiden's initiation fight. Use a distinct, forceful ensemble voice.
- **B01C02_CREW_MEMBER_2** — Gender not independently specified; one of the crew near the cars. Alarmed about the Cadillac's paint, with a sharp comic outburst distinct from the first crew member.
- **B01C02_DIAMOND_WOMAN_1** — Woman in the club's back room. Flirtatious, amused, and impressed by Aiden's battered entrance.
- **B01C02_DIAMOND_WOMAN_2** — Woman in the same three-woman group. Curious and entertained when asking whether Aiden beat Tank. The text does not establish whether she is the first woman, so the voices remain separate.
- **B01C02_VALET** — Gender unspecified. Professional concern on seeing Aiden's blood, then immediately redirected toward the car.
- **B01C02_RED_GUARD_1** — Man (“one of Red's guys”; later “he”). Confrontational gatekeeper at the elevator, addressing Shawna past Aiden.
- **B01C02_MOTEL_CLERK** — Gender unspecified. Wary surprise at Aiden paying cash for all six rooms.
- **B01C02_CALLER** — Man (“he said”) on the telephone after Big T's death. Cautious, newly deferential, waiting for Aiden's first order.

## Chapter 3

- **B01C03_CLUB_ELDER** — Older man from Big T's club. Practical accounts messenger under pressure; controlled and wary, with the rhythms of someone used to reporting to a boss.
- **B01C03_NURSE** — Woman, confirmed by Aiden's later “She saved my ass first.” Dryly unimpressed by the delivered coffin before shifting into emergency care.
- **B01C03_GUNMAN_1** — Man at the stairwell. Brief mocking confidence when he sees wounded Aiden in a wheelchair.
- **B01C03_PATIENT** — Older man, described as having lived sixty years, with one leg in a cast. Steady gallows humor followed by exuberant support for Aiden.
- **B01C03_DELIVERY_GUNMAN** — Man below the atrium, one of the false coffin delivery team. Defiant at first, then terse and compliant after losing the fight.
- **B01C03_CREW_DRIVER** — Man inferred from the group of men beside the six black cars. Respectful, understated welcome as he opens the Mercedes door.

The opening emergency-room doctor is assigned **DR_VEGA** for continuity: the chapter soon introduces Dr. Marisol Vega as Aiden's physician, and no second doctor is distinguished. This remains a small textual uncertainty because the first exchange occurs before her named introduction.

## Chapter 4

- **B01C04_REAL_ESTATE_AGENT** — Woman (“real estate woman”). Polished and increasingly careful while Aiden and Tank treat the expensive viewing like an immediate purchase.
- **LEON** — Named man, thirty-seven, steakhouse owner, and Porsche's rejected suitor/companion. Affluent, emotionally open, good-humored enough to laugh at himself, then panicked about his Bentley.
- **B01C04_DEALER** — Man trying to impress Rosa. Defensive pride that collapses quickly when Tank answers him.
- **ROMAN_PIKE** — Named man, forty-two, Brock's cousin. Entitled local enforcer performing inherited authority; forceful and transactional before the fight, humiliated afterward. Keep him grounded rather than melodramatic.
- **B01C04_DANCER** — Woman, confirmed by “She held it against her chest.” Warm, celebratory recognition of Aiden at the newly reopened club.
- **B01C04_PRETTY_MAN** — Man in a silver shirt, Aiden's former lover. Stylish, confident, teasing, and socially unruffled when Shawna stakes her claim.
- **B01C04_WHISPERING_GUEST** — Man (“He hadn't said it to hurt me”). Quiet recognition of Shawna's song as Dante's, spoken with sincerity rather than provocation.
- **B01C04_CASH_TABLE_MAN** — Man who grabs Rosa after she refuses more work. Possessive and aggrieved; a brief antagonist voice without glamor or comic exaggeration.

## Boundary notes

- All unquoted narration remains **AIDEN** and is outside these quote-only maps.
- Multi-span paragraphs retain one speaker unless the prose explicitly changes speakers. This includes Big T's split lines, Shawna's reminiscences, Roman's introductions and demands, Aiden's “My future” correction, and Shawna's final two-span explanation in Chapter 4.
- No printed sign, written message, or italicized internal wording appears in the supplied quotation templates for these chapters. Italicized written material such as the cross inscription and Big T's motel text therefore does not create a new voice assignment.
