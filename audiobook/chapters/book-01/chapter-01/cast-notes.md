# Chapter 1 dialogue cast

Source boundary: `book-01.md`, Chapter 1, lines 3–566. Aiden is the first-person narrator. This sheet covers distinct dialogue voices; it does not add spoken material.

## Continuing/core voices

- **AIDEN** — Man; first-person narrator and protagonist. Conversational authority, dry timing, quick shifts from amused observation to protective aggression. Narration and his spoken dialogue use the same identity, with dialogue allowed more immediate energy.
- **TANK** — Man; Aiden's brother. Big social presence, affectionate comic timing, emotionally sincere under the jokes. Comfortable addressing a room and pivoting into action.
- **SHAWNA** — Woman; Aiden's wife. Controlled, elegant, strategically decisive, and intimate without becoming soft-spoken. Her shortest lines should land with command.
- **MERCEDES** — Woman; recording artist whose masters are at stake. Focused and emotionally contained, with warmth toward Aiden and clear resolve around her music.
- **TICKET** — Man (referred to as “the man taking tickets”). Dry, unhurried, transactional delivery; treats the absurd funeral rules as ordinary policy.
- **GUEST** — Man (the guest behind Aiden at the entrance). Opportunistic, impressed, phone already out; comic eagerness rather than menace.

## Additional speaking voices

- **SILAS** — Man; antagonist in a white fur coat. Polished public charm with entitlement underneath; theatrical at the microphone, then sharp and reactive when control breaks. Avoid a cartoon-villain growl.
- **COOK** — Man (masculine address in dialogue/context). Practical kitchen worker with casual concern for Aiden, later pulled onto the dance floor. Grounded, direct, and unimpressed by the impostor claiming catering.
- **DRUMMER** — Man (“he” in the text). Working musician with precise deadpan timing and pride in his vintage cymbal. Calm until his gear is damaged.
- **CORPSE_PERFORMER** — Man (“little man” in a gold tracksuit). Hired for a timed resurrection-and-dance package. Loud, indignant, professional about his gig terms, and physically fearless once revealed.
- **SILAS_SMALL_MAN** — Man (one of Silas's four men; repeatedly called the smallest man). Carries the silver case, accepts shrimp, then tries an implausible catering cover. Nervous, subdued, and calculating his exit.
- **SOUND_TECH** — Young man, twenty-three, with a small beard and headphones. Curious, slightly tentative, then comfortable enough to sit and listen to the story.
- **GUEST_TABLE_WOMAN** — Woman at a table who punctures Silas's tribute with a shouted joke. Bold room-level heckle with delighted crowd confidence.
- **GUEST_AISLE_WOMAN** — Woman near the aisle. Startled “Lord,” then pragmatic urgency when Aiden approaches carrying shrimp; whispered line still needs clear comic timing.
- **GUEST_AISLE_MAN** — Man beside the aisle woman. Has just crossed himself; answers her with sincere, understated uncertainty.
- **GUEST_DANCE_WOMAN** — Woman who takes the cook's tray and pulls him onto the floor. Playful, decisive, already caught in the music.
