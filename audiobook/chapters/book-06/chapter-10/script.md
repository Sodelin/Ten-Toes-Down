# 10. My Wife Said Get Down from There

"Brother!" "What?" "Don't scratch the wedding!" We rose above the ridge. The fortress spread beneath us: black walls, gold roofs, courtyards full of armored vehicles, fountains, runways, towers. It skidded onto the runway, sparks spraying beneath its belly, and came to a grinding stop against the far sand bank. The pilot crawled out, alive and waving both hands. Victor landed beside him, breathing hard. The pilot stared. "You saved me." Victor pointed at our car.

"That nigga taught me." I heard him through the open window and smiled so hard Shawna shook her head. "You don't need more encouragement." "Nigga, that's between me and my public." We reached the buses under fire from the outer court.

The guards beside them looked at the police lights, the wrecked plane, the airship descending above us, and the woman stepping out of my car with a badge inside her jacket. Shawna raised her pistol.
